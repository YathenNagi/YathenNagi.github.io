Financial models I've built, from valuation to deal analysis.

WD-40 Valuation Model

Excel · DCF · Comparable companies

Built a full three-statement model and discounted cash flow valuation for WD-40 (WDFC) with a 6.2% WACC and 2% terminal growth. The DCF estimated a value of about $154 per share against a $221 market price, which supported a sell rating. I cross-checked the result with dividend discount models, trading and transaction comps, and an analysis at various prices.

Leveraged Buyout Analysis

Excel · LBO · Returns analysis

Modeled a €450M buyout at 10x EBITDA, funded with senior debt, seller's notes, a shareholder loan, management rollover, and sponsor equity. With a five-year cash sweep, the sponsor earns about a 19% IRR and 2.4x money multiple at a 10x exit. I tested exit multiples and leverage levels and recommended the deal.
